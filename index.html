Privacy

Private by design.

Parallel stores your journal on your device, and can privately sync it across your Apple devices using your own iCloud account. It's your data — we built Parallel with one rule: keep it that way.

Optional Face ID / Touch ID

Lock the app behind biometric authentication on supported devices, on top of your device's own passcode.

Your iCloud, not our servers

Sync is optional and uses your own private iCloud account. Your entries aren't stored on any server we run.

No ads, no tracking

No advertising, no third-party analytics, and nothing sold. Parallel doesn't use any third-party SDKs at all.

No account required

Use Parallel fully offline with no sign-up. iCloud sync is opt-in and uses the Apple ID already on your device.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Parallel doesn't have a custom account system. You can use it fully offline. iCloud sync is optional and simply uses the Apple ID already signed in on your device.

Is my journal actually private?

Yes. Your entries are stored on your device, and if you enable sync, in your own private iCloud account — not on any server we operate. We don't sell or share your data, and Parallel doesn't use advertising or third-party analytics.

Does Parallel support Face ID or Touch ID?

Yes. You can require Face ID or Touch ID to open the app, in addition to your device's own lock screen security.

How do backups and restoring work?

Use Settings to export a full backup, including photos and exact dates, or export your entries as Markdown, PDF, or JSON. Import a backup anytime to restore your journal on a new device.

I already bought unlimited partners. Do I have to pay again on my other device?

No. Use Restore Purchases in Settings on any device signed in with the same Apple ID.

Is Relationship Mode only for polyamory or ENM?

Not at all. Relationship Mode works for anyone dating casually, practicing ethical non-monogamy, exploring polyamory, or in a single long-term relationship — really, anyone who wants their journal organized around the people in their life, not just the calendar.

What devices does Parallel work on?

iPhone and Mac, with entries syncing privately between them through your own iCloud account. iPad support isn't available yet.
